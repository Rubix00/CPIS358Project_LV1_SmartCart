How The Demo Works

In the demo screen, you will get two options that read "Milk" and "Bread", Each with their respective code and price. Once you add the code, the item will be added to your list and and it's price will be shown, as will as the overall price of all items added into your list! You can also remove the items from the list if you decide that you don't want to buy them any more.
