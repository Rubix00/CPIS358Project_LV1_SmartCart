How we came up with the idea

It all started on the final day of a group project idea submission. Khalid and Abdullmohsen each found themselves with neither a group nor an idea, but when they found out that the other person also wasn't in a group, they made the only obvious decision and decided to partner up!

After they had finished their classes, they had spent hours upon hours of that Thursday trying to come up with something, but to no avail. And after tiring mental strain, they decided to go to a supermarket, buy some food and drinks, take a nice little break, and then get back to work before the deadline.

After having reached the supermarket and gathering everything they wanted to buy, they went to the cashiers to buy their items, planning on finding somewhere quiet afterwards to have their break and start brainstorming again. But, once they reached the cashier, they were met with such an absurdly long line! they waited and waited, but the line was moving so slowly. Having barely any patience left, Abdullmohsen looks at Khalid and says:
"This line is so long! At this rate we will lose so much valuable time, and we haven't even agreed on an idea yet!
Khalid looks at Abdullmohsen and agrees, proceeding to say:
"Yeah the line is way too slow...I wish we had a better system than this. If only there was a way for us to bypass all this wait..."

And just then, Khalid has a Eureka! moment...

"What if we made that our project!". While looking over at Abdullmohsen."What if we had a way to reduce all this wasted time at supermarkets and make the customer experience better and faster!"
After a brief pause... Abdullmohsen looks back at Khalid and says...
"Khalid...you are a genius!"

And the rest is history...

What our project is about

Introduction

Every store seeks to provide the best shopping experience for their customers, especially by using technologies that advance that experience from the boring old chore that it is, but despite all their best efforts, stores still face difficulty in one of the biggest hassles facing the customers…improving the payment process at the cashier. One of the most prominent of issues is the crowding at cashier areas, which forces customers to wait for a long time, especially during peak times. The reason for this problem is the time it takes to calculate the total for the products, which is only made worse when customers intend to purchase a huge number of items. Is there a way for the customers and cashiers to know the total payment costs, and perhaps even paying, before reaching the sales box?

The problem we want to fix

Despite the technological advancements that exist at the present time, one of the most important challenges facing stores is the crowding at the sales counters, which leads to long waiting times, as well as the problem of not knowing the total amount you might have to pay until arriving at the payment box, and this may cause embarrassment to the customer if they find out they don’t have enough money to pay.

Our solution to the problem

The solution we propose is to create a smart cart that works with built-in sensors that recognize products as soon as you put them in the shopping cart. It also contains a digital screen in the handlebar that displays the items in the cart, prices of each product in the cart, the total, and finally an electronic payment system built into the cart which allows you to pay without having to wait in line. This solution saves you from standing in a long payment queue and shows you the value of the products in your cart without waiting to reach the cashier.
